Privacy notice

EVE Librarian - last updated 7 November 2026

Who uses it

EVE Librarian is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive in order to organise and index them. It does not access other users' data.

Where data goes

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive. Working copies are processed on the owner's own server and are not shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services.
